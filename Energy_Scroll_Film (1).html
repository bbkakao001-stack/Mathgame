8.3 · ENERGY MEASUREMENT

Energy is measured
in Joules.

The SI unit of energy. One joule = work done when 1 N moves an object 1 m. Daily amounts use kilojoules (kJ).

CONVERSION
1 kJ = 1000 J

6.1 · ENERGY TRANSFER

A change is a clue.

Energy is transferred when something changes. Look for observable evidence instead of saying that energy simply appeared or disappeared.

MotionA trolley speeds up, slows down or changes direction.
TemperatureWater warms, or a surface gets hotter.
Other evidenceA lamp gives light, a buzzer makes sound, or an elastic object changes shape.
6.1 · SPOT THE CHANGE

When nothing changes.

A stationary object remaining stationary does not, by itself, show that energy is being transferred. Look for a change or another piece of evidence.

Not enough evidenceA book resting on a table stays still.
New evidenceIf the book is lifted, its height changes and energy is transferred.

6.2 · USEFUL AND WASTED

Useful depends on the task.

Useful energy is transferred in the way we want. Wasted energy is transferred in an unwanted way for that task.

Task: light a roomLight is useful; heating the surroundings is usually wasted.
Task: heat foodEnergy transferred to the food is useful; sound escaping from the oven is wasted.
Ask “useful for what?”The same type of energy can be useful in one task and unwanted in another.

6.2 · DISSIPATION

Energy spreads out.

When energy transfers to the surroundings and becomes more spread out, it is described as dissipated. It has not disappeared.

BeforeEnergy is concentrated in a store or transfer that can make a chosen change happen.
AfterSome energy spreads to the surroundings as heating, sound or vibration.
Less usefulThe energy is harder to use to make the original change happen again.
6.2 · CONSERVATION

Wasted does not mean gone.

Energy is not destroyed when it is dissipated. It is transferred to the surroundings and becomes less useful for the intended task.

Account for energyInput energy is transferred to useful outputs and less-useful outputs.
Check the languageSay “dissipated to the surroundings”, not “used up” or “vanished”.
