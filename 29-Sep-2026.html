8.3 · ENERGY MEASUREMENT

Energy is measured
in Joules.

The SI unit of energy. One joule = work done when 1 N moves an object 1 m. Daily amounts use kilojoules (kJ).

CONVERSION
1 kJ = 1000 J

8.3 · SANKEY DIAGRAMS

Sankey shows
energy transfers.

Input = Useful + Wasted. Width of arrows = amount of energy. Input enters left, useful continues right, wasted branches down.

RULE
Wasted = Input − Useful
⚡ QUICK QUIZ

6.1 · DOING WORK

Work = Force ×
Distance.

When a force causes an object to move, energy is transferred. Scientists call this "doing work".

FORMULA
W = F × d

6.2 · ENERGY DISSIPATION

Useful vs
wasted energy.

Every useful transfer has some less-useful transfer. When energy spreads into surroundings, it is dissipated.

✅

USEFUL

Light, motion, heat cooking food

❌

WASTED

Heat to surroundings, sound, vibration
